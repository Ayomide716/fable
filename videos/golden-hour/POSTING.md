# Golden Hour Motel: how to post it

**The file:** `renders/golden-hour-tiktok.mp4`. It's 30 s long, 1080×1920 at 24 fps, and has no sound on purpose.

## Add the song in TikTok

1. Upload the video, then tap **Add sound** and search **"Cinnamon Girl"** (Lana Del Rey).
2. Open the sound's trim tool and set it to start at **0:36.8**.
3. To check the sync, watch **5.1 s** into the video. The neon MOTEL sign should snap on exactly
   as the song swells back in, right after the short pause.
4. If it's slightly early or late, nudge the trim. Every other cut follows from that one point.
